PREPAREDNESS ISN'T PARANOIA. IT'S CAPABILITY.

Store water safely.

Use food-grade containers intended for water, follow local public-health recommendations, and rotate stored water as directed. Filtration and disinfection are not interchangeable, and not every method removes every contaminant.

Things worth checking today

THE SMALL DETAILS THAT CHANGE EVERYTHING.

Readiness often comes from simple habits repeated before they are urgently needed.

01

Offline contacts

Keep important emergency numbers written down as well as saved in your phone.

02

Check the basics

Test lights, inspect batteries, and review expiry dates on a regular schedule.

03

Know your exits

Identify more than one safe way out and discuss a meeting point with your household.

04

Practice first

Learn equipment instructions and try routine tasks before a stressful situation.
